# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want img tools to be a welcoming project where people can contribute without
harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when giving feedback.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful impact.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on the work.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's information.
- Help make the project accessible to people with different experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, deliberate
disruption, doxxing, or sustained personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Do not open a public issue about a conduct incident. Contact the maintainer
privately through the contact method listed on
[@mbianchidev's GitHub profile](https://github.com/mbianchidev). Include relevant
links or screenshots and the outcome you are seeking.

Reports will be reviewed confidentially. The maintainer may remove content,
restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant when needed to protect
the community.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone represents the project in
public.
